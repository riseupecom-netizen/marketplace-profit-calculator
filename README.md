# Marketplace Profit Calculator

A small, browser-based tool for estimating the contribution from one marketplace order. Enter your own costs; the calculator does not assume or hard-code marketplace fee rates.

## Use it

Open [index.html](index.html) in a current web browser. No account, installation, build step, or external service is required. The sample values are only an example; replace them with figures that apply to your order.

## Inputs

- Selling price before discount and discount funded by you
- Product cost and packaging
- Shipping or fulfilment
- Marketplace fees, entered as an amount from your current estimate or statement
- Advertising allocated to the order
- Expected return or damage allowance
- Other variable costs

## Calculation

- Net sales = max(0, selling price − seller-funded discount)
- Total costs = product + packaging + fulfilment + fees + allocated ads + return allowance + other variable costs
- Estimated contribution = net sales − total costs
- Contribution margin = estimated contribution ÷ net sales × 100 (0% when net sales are zero)

The estimate runs in your browser. Values are not uploaded or saved. This is a planning aid, not an official marketplace fee quote or accounting record. Fee rules, taxes, shipping and return charges vary; verify current amounts against your account statements and adviser before making decisions.
